Dryer Repair in Oceanside

Clothes still damp, dryer not heating or drum not turning? We repair gas and electric dryers of every major brand. Locally owned since 1996, available 7 days a week — including evenings, weekends and holidays.

Request a repair (760) 754-8200

Common problems we fix

  • Not heating or taking too long to dry
  • Drum not turning
  • Squealing, thumping or scraping noises
  • Shuts off mid-cycle
  • Won’t start
  • Overheating or burning smell (turn it off and call us)

Not on the list? Describe what’s happening and we’ll let you know if we can help.
